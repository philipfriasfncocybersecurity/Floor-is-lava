# Floor Is Lava 3D

A 3D "the floor is lava" browser game for up to 4 players online. No sign-in needed.

**Play:** https://philipfriasfncocybersecurity.github.io/floor-is-lava/

## How to play with family and friends
1. Open the link, type your name, and tap **Join online room**.
2. Tap **Copy invite link** and send it to the others (up to 4 players).
3. They open the link, type their names, and tap Join.
4. The first person in the room is the host and taps **Start**.

Platforms crack and sink into the lava. Jump to safe ones and shove other players off. Last one standing wins.

**Phone:** drag on the left side to move, drag on the right side to look, and use the JUMP and PUSH buttons.
**Computer:** WASD to move, Space to jump, E to shove, drag the mouse to look.

Players connect through free public MQTT relays (EMQX and HiveMQ). Room codes are not secret, so use the random code the game gives you.
